Privacy Policy

Ne Giysem? (“What should I wear?”) needs no account. Your settings, saved places, routine and wardrobe stay on your device. This page explains which data goes where, why, and how to delete it. It is also the information notice required by Article 10 of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If the Turkish and English versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

In short

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your settings, places, “Günüm” (My day) routine, profiles and “Dolabım” (My wardrobe) list never reach us.
  • Location only for the weather. Your location, or the coordinates of a place you pick, go to our own server to look up the weather and the place name. The server rounds them before passing them to the weather and map services, and keeps no location history.
  • Ads and affiliate links. The Android app shows Google AdMob ads and the website shows Google AdSense ads. Some clothing items have “Buy / affiliate link” links. All of them are clearly labelled.
  • Delete with one button. “Delete all my data” wipes everything on your device.

2What data we process

Data that stays on your device

  • Your settings: how you feel the cold (“I get cold easily”, “I run warm”), your activity (city, outdoors, sport), how long you are out, language and appearance.
  • Your saved places: the name you give them (e.g. “Home”, “School”) and their coordinates.
  • Your “Günüm” (My day) routine: your trips, times, days, and walking, waiting and public transport steps.
  • Your profiles (e.g. a child profile) and the clothes you mark as owned in “Dolabım” (My wardrobe).
  • Your packing list choices, a dismissed rain alert, the last weather received (so the app works offline) and your last found location.

On the website this is kept in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), and the app files are cached by the browser for offline use. In the Android app it is kept in the app’s own storage; the home-screen widget keeps the selected place, your settings and routine in a separate file inside the app. The Android app’s data is excluded from Google backup. We cannot see any of it; only the coordinates needed for the weather reach our server (see below).

Data sent to our server for the weather and place names

The app gets the weather, place names and search results from our own server (a small backend running on Cloudflare Pages). To do so it sends:

  • Coordinates: your device location (if you allow it) or the coordinates of a place you picked or saved; if you have a routine, the coordinates of your trip places; if you prepare a packing list, the coordinates of your destination.
  • Place search text: what you type into the search box, plus an approximate location to sort results by distance.
  • Connection data: as with any internet request, your IP address, the time of the request and your browser or device information (User-Agent).

If you do not allow location and have no saved place, your approximate location (city level) is estimated from your IP address by our hosting provider Cloudflare and is used only to find the weather.

Other data

  • Ads: advertising ID or cookies, IP address, device and browser information, and your interactions with ads (by Google; see section 6).
  • Affiliate links: the affiliate code in the link when you tap one (section 7).
  • Messages: your email address and message if you email us.
  • Problem reports: “For you → Report a problem” opens your email app; you write and send the email yourself. The app adds these technical details: the app version, your phone model and Android version (on the website, your browser details), language, screen size, a few settings (for example “I get cold easily”, the number of trips), when the weather was last fetched, the widget’s last problem and the latest error and crash records the app keeps on this device. Your location, places and routine are never added; numbers that look like coordinates are removed from the records. You can see and change everything before sending. Error and crash records stay on your device only, at most the latest 15 errors and 5 crashes; “Delete all my data” deletes them too.
  • Fonts and icons: they are the site’s and the app’s own files. Opening a page sends no request to any other site (such as Google Fonts).
  • Test programme (Google Play closed test): if you join the test group (Google Groups) to take part in the app’s closed test on Google Play, we see your Google account’s email address and the name shown on it. Group members cannot see each other. Google Play shows us the number of testers and the private feedback you leave through Play. We use this only to run the test, to meet Google Play’s testing requirement and to answer your feedback; we do not share it with anyone or use it for ads or marketing.
  • Google Play: when you install the app from Google Play, Google processes its own data under its own privacy policy. The Play Console shows us aggregate statistics, such as install counts and crash reports, that do not identify you.

The app has no analytics or tracking tools of our own. We do not process special categories of personal data such as health or beliefs.

3How location is used

  • Only when you ask. Location is requested when you open the app, refresh the weather, or tap “Save where I am now”. On Android, “Approximate” location permission is enough. Without location permission you can still search for and pick a place, or set the weather by hand.
  • No background location. The app never checks your location in the background. If you add a home-screen widget, it refreshes the weather about every 30 minutes using the saved coordinates of the place you last used in the app (or, if there is none yet, an approximate location estimated from your IP address). The refresh stops when you remove the last widget. If you turn on weather warning notifications (“For you” → Weather warnings), the same refresh keeps running even without a widget; the phone itself works out dangerous weather from the forecast and shows the notification, and nothing extra is sent for it. Turning the notifications off (with no widget) stops the refresh.
  • Rounded on our server. Our server rounds coordinates to cells of about 1 km (2 decimal places) for the weather and to about 100 m (3 decimal places) for place names and search sorting. Only these rounded values and the search text are sent to MET Norway and Photon, under our server’s own name; your IP address and device information are not passed on.
  • Shared cache. To reuse results for everyone in the same area, forecasts and place names are cached at Cloudflare by rounded coordinates (and searches by search text): forecasts for a few hours, searches for a few days, place names for up to two months. These entries are not linked to you or your device.
  • No location history. We do not write incoming coordinates to a database and keep no location history. Our hosting provider Cloudflare keeps its own standard request logs to run and protect the service (section 9).

Showing and measuring ads, preventing ad fraud

5Recipients

Cloudflare, Inc. (USA)

Hosts the website and the server functions. Your IP address, connection data, the coordinates sent and search text pass through this infrastructure; Cloudflare can derive an approximate location from the IP address. It works on our behalf and on our instructions (processor).

MET Norway — Norwegian Meteorological Institute (Norway)

Provides the forecast. It receives only coordinates rounded to about 1 km, via our server; your IP address is not passed on. Weather data © MET Norway, CC BY 4.0.

komoot GmbH — Photon (Germany)

Provides place search and place names. It receives the search text and coordinates rounded to about 100 m, via our server; your IP address is not passed on. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Your smartwatch (Wear OS) — Google Play services

If you install the Ne Giysem? watch app, the phone sends the summary its widget shows (place name, temperature, outfit suggestion, any weather warning) to your paired watch, which shows it in its complication and on its own screen. This goes through Google Play services’ watch connection: when the watch isn’t connected to the phone by Bluetooth, the data may travel through Google’s servers. The watch keeps only the latest summary; “Delete all my data” deletes it too.

When our server can't be reached: MET Norway, Photon and BigDataCloud (Australia) directly

On some networks our server’s address (pages.dev) can be blocked. Then, for half an hour, the app and the widget send the same requests to these services directly instead of through our server: coordinates rounded to about 1 km to MET Norway for the weather, the search text and rough location to Photon for place search, and coordinates rounded to about 100 m to BigDataCloud for place names and a rough location. In that case these services also see your IP address. Nothing else is added; the weather is worked out on the phone with the same code our server uses.

Google (Google LLC, USA; Google Ireland Limited for users in Europe)

AdMob ads in the Android app, AdSense ads on the website, Google Play for distributing the app, and Google Groups for the closed test group. Google also processes ad and Play data under its own privacy policy and responsibility.

Google Maps (Routes API), only if you turn route suggestions on: the start and end coordinates of your trips and their departure times go to Google through our server (your IP address is not passed on). The routes in Google’s answer are only shown while the page is open and are stored neither on your device nor on our server. By using this feature you agree to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

GitHub, Inc. (USA)

Only if you tap “Download the app” on the website: the Android app’s file (APK) is downloaded from GitHub, which then sees your IP address and browser information and processes them under its own privacy statement.

Trendyol and Amazon.com.tr

Only when you tap an affiliate link: the shop opens in your browser, and what you do there is covered by that shop’s privacy policy.

Competent public authorities

Only where the law requires it, and only as much as required.

International transfers

Cloudflare, Google (including route suggestions and the test group), MET Norway, komoot, BigDataCloud and GitHub run servers outside Türkiye (in the USA, Europe and other countries), so using the app transfers the data above abroad. These transfers rely on the safeguards listed in Article 9 of KVKK and Chapter V of the GDPR (such as the standard contractual clauses offered by the providers) or, where none is available, on the exceptions those rules allow. The rounded coordinates sent to MET Norway and Photon are not sent together with anything that identifies you.

Website (Google AdSense)

Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure views and clicks, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise ads to your interests. On your first visit you are asked whether you allow personalised ads. You can reopen and change that choice at any time with the “Reklam tercihleri” (Ad preferences) link at the bottom of the web app. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads.

  • How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Ad cookies: How Google uses cookies in advertising
  • Manage ad personalisation for your Google Account: My Ad Center

Android app (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Local storage

The app itself sets no cookies. It uses local storage to keep your settings on your device and the browser cache to work offline; both are needed for the service and never leave your device.

9How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: until you delete it (“Delete all my data”, uninstalling the app or clearing site data).
  • Coordinates and search text sent to the server: not kept after the request is answered; only the shared cache entries, which are not linked to anyone, remain for the periods in section 3.
  • Cloudflare’s request logs: for Cloudflare’s own retention periods; we have no logging system of our own.
  • Request counter for route suggestions: to limit abuse, an hourly counter is kept per visitor. Its name is a code made from the IP address with a secret key, which cannot be turned back and changes every hour; the IP address itself is not stored. The counter lasts at most 1 hour.
  • Test programme: your membership of the test group lasts until you leave or until the group is deleted, at the latest 30 days after the test ends and the app is released to everyone; feedback for Google Play’s own retention periods.
  • Ad data: for the periods set out in Google’s policies.
  • Emails: up to 12 months after the conversation ends, or longer where the law requires it.

Data whose period has ended is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

10Deleting your data

  • In the app: in the “Sana göre” (For you) card, under “Verilerim” (My data), tap “Tüm verilerimi sil” (Delete all my data) twice. Your settings, places, routine, profiles, wardrobe, packing list and last weather are deleted; on Android the widget’s data is cleared too.
  • On Android: uninstalling the app, or Settings → Apps → Ne Giysem? → Storage → Clear data, also deletes everything.
  • On the web: clear this site’s data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (section 6).
  • On our server: we keep no account or record that can be linked to you, so there is nothing to delete. If you still have a request, write to saawanco@gmail.com.

11Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Under Article 11 of KVKK you can ask the controller to tell you whether your data is processed and, if so, to give you information about it; to learn the purpose and whether the data is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it was transferred to; to correct incomplete or inaccurate data; to delete or destroy it under Article 7; to notify those third parties of such corrections or deletions; to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply: email saawanco@gmail.com, or use the other methods set out in Türkiye’s Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. We answer within 30 days, free of charge as a rule. Most of your data exists only on your device, so we cannot see it; you can delete it yourself as described in section 10.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK). If you live in the EU or EEA, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

12Children

Ne Giysem? is meant for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. The child profile in the app helps parents dress their children for the weather, and its details stay on the device. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

13Security

All connections between the app and the server are encrypted with HTTPS. There are no accounts or passwords, and we process as little data as possible. Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security (such as the screen lock); keep this in mind if you share your device.

  • Only its own code: through a Content Security Policy, the website and the app’s screen (its page) run only their own code and can connect only to the services listed in this policy; they cannot send data to any other site, and injected code cannot run. (AdMob ads and Google Play services in the Android app work outside the page; see section 6.)
  • Not shown inside other sites: our pages cannot be opened in a frame on another site, and your browser does not tell other sites which of our pages you came from.
  • Android app: the app’s screen opens only its own built-in files; links open in your browser. Location permission is given only to the app’s own page. The app’s data is not included in Google backups.
  • Server keys: the Google key used for route suggestions stays on the server and never reaches the app or the page.
  • Reporting a vulnerability: if you find a security issue, please report it to saawanco@gmail.com before making it public.
